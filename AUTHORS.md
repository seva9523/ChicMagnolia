# Authors and project stewardship

Chic Magnolia was founded and is maintained by
[Sevinj Ahmadova](https://github.com/seva9523). The product combines post-purchase opportunity
intelligence with privacy-conscious account controls, automated retailer monitoring, and a
subscription-ready SaaS architecture.

Professional and research background is available on
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sevinj-ahmadova-imc-240738131/).

## Contribution and authorship principles

- Material code and documentation contributions should retain their Git attribution.
- AI-assisted changes require human review, repository tests, and accurate public disclosure where
  the contribution policy or pull-request context calls for it.
- Product adoption, commercial results, and external recognition are not inferred from repository
  activity alone; they should be supported by independently verifiable evidence.
- Security or privacy reports should follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not public issue threads.
